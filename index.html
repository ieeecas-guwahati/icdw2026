About

A three-day hands-on workshop on integrated circuit design, organised by the IEEE Kolkata Section Chapter, CAS04-Guwahati, in collaboration with the IEEE CASS Student Branch Chapter, IIT Guwahati.

The workshop series aims to build practical, hands-on skills in analog IC design and digital VLSI design, with a particular focus on widening access to advanced VLSI training for students and researchers in North-East India. By working with industry-standard design tools, analysing circuits at the transistor level and exploring HDL-based digital design, participants will see how classroom concepts connect to the way semiconductor design is practised today.

Over three days, participants begin with MOSFET characteristics and device-level simulation, move on to CMOS inverter design and layout, and finish with the design of common source and differential amplifiers. Each topic opens with a short lecture and is followed by a guided hands-on session using tools such as Cadence Virtuoso and LTspice, covering DC, AC and transient analysis along the way.
